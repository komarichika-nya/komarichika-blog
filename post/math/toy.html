Yong inequality

Theorem 2 (Yong inequality). define p,q>1\land \frac{1}{p}+\frac{1}{q}=1,u,v\ge0 \frac{u^p}{p}+\frac{v^q}{q}\ge uv

Proof. when u,v=0 obviously established.
when u,v>0. According Jensen’s inequality :
\frac{1}{p}\log(u^p)+\frac{1}{q}\log(v^q)\le\log(\frac{u^p}{p}+\frac{v^q}{q}) (u^p)^{\frac{1}{p}}(v^q)^{\frac{1}{q}}\le\frac{u^p}{p}+\frac{v^q}{q} ◻
