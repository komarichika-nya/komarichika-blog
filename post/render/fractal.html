The Holomorphic function is defined as: \{U\subseteq\mathbb{C},f:U\to\mathbb{C}:\forall z_0\in U,\exists\lim_{h\to0\land h\in\mathbb{C}}\frac{f(z_0+h)-f(z_0)}{h}\} The Univalent function is defined as: \{U\subseteq\mathbb{C},f:U\to\mathbb{C}:f is Holomorphic\land\forall z_1,z_2\in U(z_1\ne z_2\implies f(z_1)\ne f(z_2))\} Obviously, Holomorphic is equal to analytic.
