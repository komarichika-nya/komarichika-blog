This discussion focuses only on the Julia and Mandelbrot fractal sets. For most fractals, we define constants $c$ and $z_n$ as points on the complex plane. If a point satisfies the function $z_{n+1}=f(z_n)$, it is called a fractal. In the Julia and Mandelbrot sets, we often use escape-time algorithms to calculate fractal trajectories. For a point $z_n$ on the complex plane, during the iteration process of $|z_{n+1}|=|{z_n}|^2+c$, an escape occurs if and only if $|z_n|>2\land|z_n|>=c$. Why this condition? Let's provide a simple proof: the core is to prove that when the above condition is met, the iteration diverges and increases to infinity. First, from the triangle inequality, we clearly have: $$ |z_n|>=c\\[10pt]|z_n|^2-|c|>=|z_{n}|^2-|z_n| $$ Simple scaling yields: $$ |z_{n+1}|>=|z_n|^2-|z_n|\\[10pt]|z_{n+1}|>=|z_n|(|z_n|-1) $$ because: $$ |z_n|>2\\[10pt]|z_n|-1>1 $$ so$\forall k>=0$: $$ |z_{n+1}|>|z_n| $$ suppose$z_n$If it is bounded and convergent, let its limit be $x$, then: $$ x>=x^2-x $$ Solving for x, we get $x <= 0$, which is a contradiction. Therefore, $\lim_{n\to\infty}|z_n| =\infty$, which holds true. If we directly apply the escape-time algorithm to draw the fractal image, we'll find obvious color banding. Let's try smoothing the color and take a closer look at this iterative formula. $$ |z_{n+1}|=|z_{n}|^2+c\\[10pt] $$ When $n \to \infty$, the effect of the constant term can be ignored: $$ |z_n|\approx|z_0|^{2^n}\\[10pt] $$ Taking the logarithm of both sides again: $$ \log |z_n|\approx 2^n\log |z_0|\\[10pt]\log\log |z_n|\approx n+\log\log |z_0|\\[10pt]n\approx\log_2(\frac{\log |z_n|}{\log |z_0|})\\[10pt]n\approx\log_2\log|z_n| $$ Rendering Fractal Images Using Ray Tracing Algorithm The ray tracing algorithm emits a ray towards an object, finds the intersection point, and determines whether the intersection point is the object or the background. However, fractals don't have simple equations. We can trace along the ray to find the nearest point from our current position. Fortunately, such a distance estimation function exists, which we will derive below. Starting from the iterative formula above: $$ |z_n|\approx|z_0|^{2^n}\\[10pt]\ln|z_n|\approx2^n\ln|z_0|\\[10pt]\frac{\ln|z_n|}{2^n}\approx\ln|z_0| $$ Define $G(z)=\frac{\ln|z_n|}{2^n}$, then the distance $d=\frac{G(z)}{|\nabla G(z)|}$, so: $$ \nabla G(z)\approx\frac{1}{2^n}\frac{|z_n'|}{|z_n|}\\[10pt]d\approx\frac{|z_n|\ln|z_n|}{|z_n'|} $$
