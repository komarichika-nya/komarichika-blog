\subsubsection{Chapter I}
To me. the back of the old dormitory was a rare "hidden gem".
\\
\\
"Hidden gem" or not, environment-wise the place left a lot to be desired. Rumor had it school was planning to renovate it. That was what they said, anyway-but years had passed without a single hammer being swung. After class, delinquents gathered there to smoke away from the teachers' eyes, while students in the dorm tossed their trash out the windows. Eventaully, the stench of rotting fruit peels and cheap cigarette smoke stewed in the summer heat into a sticky film that smeared itself across the walls, truning the place into a gathering spot for delinquents and trash bags alike.
\\
\\
Because of sat in such an out-of-the way corner, the school almost never bothered to keep an eye on it.
\\
\\
I was a kind of person who liked quiet. Every day, after lunch and dinner, I'd pass through here. It meant taking a slight detour, but to me, it was a rare little sanctuary of clam.
\\
\\
The whole thing was pretty bizarre, actually.
\\
\\
That day, after finishing lunch, I was about to cut through the old dormitory on my way back to the classroom. Normally, there shoudn't have been anyone around at that hour-but reality had other plans.
\\
\\
"She looks likes Ai Suotome."
\\
\\
That was my first impression of her.
\\
\\
The only difference was that she had medium-length hair, and she was about ten centimeters shorter than me.
\\
\\
The best choice was pass by ordinarily and inconspicuously as possible, with as little presence as I could manage. I wasn't good at greeting people, either. So, while studying her movements out of conner of my eye, I forced on an air of perfect nonchalance and walked past her.
\\
\\
About a minute later, I successfully passed by in front of her while maintaining my "normal" act.
\\
\\
I went so smoothly that I mently gave myself full marks. I coudn't help but wonder if something was actually going to go wrong.
\\
\\
Well, if nothing unexpected happened something unexpected was definitely about to happen.
\\
\\
"Hey."
\\
\\
Was she call me? Or someone else?
\\
\\
Whatever is was, if I truned around now, everything would be ruined.
\\
\\
I pretended not to hear and kept walking.
\\
\\
I felt a wave of pressure bearing down on me from behind.
\\
\\
This was abslutely not my imagination.
\\
\\
She came after in long strides-right behind me.
\\
\\
"Didn't you hear me calling you just now?"
\\
\\
Her tone was forceful, but there wasn't the slightest hint of reproach in it.
\\
\\
I turned around, deliberately avoiding her gaze, unable to get a single word out.
\\
\\
But I knew-this definitely wasn't because I was afraid.
\\
\\
She wearing a yellow jacket over a white hoodie, with a small pink ribbon tied at the collar. Her bangs were long, already covering the bridge of her nose, and a little tuft was tied up on the left side of her head.
\\
\\
Yellow eyes
\\
\\
Right then there, I concluded that she was definitely a beautiful girl-and absilutely adorable, too.
\\
\\
When I gave no reponse, she sighed and start talking on her own.
\\
\\
"Hello, Yae. I'm Yuki".
\\
\\
Wait.
\\
\\
How did she know my name? This was supposed to be the first time we'd ever met, right? But the name Yuki did feel strangely familiar-like maybe I'd known her before?
\\
\\
In the end, I could't remember.
\\
\\
I listened as Yuki introduced herself without waiting for me, but still couldn't recall a single thing about her.
\\
\\
"Hello...Have we met before?"
